## Section 1: Role of Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI)
The Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) is the self-regulatory and industry body representing all SEBI-registered Asset Management Companies (AMCs). AMFI establishes best practices, code of ethics, ARN licensing for mutual fund distributors, and investor awareness programs.

## Section 2: Direct vs Regular Mutual Fund Plans
### Subsection 2.1: Statutory Distinction
- Direct Plans: Purchased directly through the AMC or registered Execution-Only Platforms (EOPs). Direct plans have lower Total Expense Ratios (TER) because no distributor commission or brokerage is paid, resulting in higher net compounded returns over the long term.
- Regular Plans: Purchased through registered Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs). The AMC pays trail commission out of the scheme expense ratio to the distributor for advisory, onboarding, and ongoing service.

## Section 3: Risk-o-meter and Portfolio Disclosure Norms
### Subsection 3.1: Dynamic Scheme Risk-o-meter
- AMCs are mandated by SEBI and AMFI to evaluate portfolio holdings monthly and assign risk ratings: Low, Low to Moderate, Moderate, Moderately High, High, and Very High.

### Subsection 3.2: Monthly Portfolio Disclosures
- All AMCs must publish complete portfolio disclosures including top 10 holdings, sector weightings, and debt instrument credit ratings within 10 days of the end of each calendar month.
