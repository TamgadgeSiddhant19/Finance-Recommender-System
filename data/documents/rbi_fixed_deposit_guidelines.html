Reserve Bank of India Master Directions on Bank Deposits and Fixed Deposits

Regulatory Body: Reserve Bank of India (RBI)

Applicability: All Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), and Small Finance Banks in India.

1. Term Deposits and Fixed Deposits (FD) Structure

A Term Deposit / Fixed Deposit is a deposit received by a bank for a fixed period withdrawable only after the expiry of the designated term or via premature penalty withdrawal terms.

  • Minimum Tenor: The minimum tenor for term deposits in commercial banks is 7 days, extending up to a maximum period of 10 years (120 months).
  • Interest Rates: Scheduled Commercial Banks determine their deposit interest rates independently with approval of their Asset Liability Management Committees (ALCO) / Board of Directors.
  • Senior Citizens: Banks are permitted to offer an additional interest rate differential (commonly 50 basis points / 0.50% p.a.) on term deposits of resident senior citizens (aged 60 years and above).

2. DICGC Deposit Insurance Protection

Every depositor in an insured bank is insured by the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Reserve Bank of India, up to a maximum of INR 5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakhs) for both principal and interest amount held in the same capacity and same right across all branches of that insured bank.

Eligible deposits covered under DICGC insurance include:

  1. Savings bank accounts
  2. Current accounts
  3. Fixed deposits (FDs)
  4. Recurring deposits (RDs)

3. Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on Bank Fixed Deposits

Under Section 194A of the Income Tax Act, 1961:

  • TDS is applicable on FD interest if total interest across all branches of a bank exceeds INR 40,000 per financial year for regular individual depositors.
  • For senior citizen resident depositors (aged 60+), the TDS threshold is enhanced to INR 50,000 per financial year under Section 194A / Section 80TTB.
  • Depositors whose estimated total income is below the taxable threshold may submit Form 15G (for regular individuals) or Form 15H (for senior citizens) to prevent TDS deduction.
